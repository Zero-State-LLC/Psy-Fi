    Safety, storage, seeds and GPUs

    Is this medical advice?
    No. It models phenomenology for research and visualization only.
    Can I calm the field fast?
    Neutral switches to a calm frame immediately.
    Is my image or camera stored?
    No. Uploads and sensor readings are never stored.
    Does the same seed repeat?
    Yes. Runs are deterministic and carry their provenance.
    Do I need a GPU?
    No. Canvas 2D always works; WebGL and WebGPU are optional.
